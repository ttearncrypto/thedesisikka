  <div class="prose" style="max-width:46rem;">
    <p>The DESI Sikka started because reading crypto news in India felt like homework. One site hypes every pump. Another buries you in jargon. A third wants your email before showing you a single fact.</p>

    <p>We do it differently.</p>

    <p>Short stories. Plain English (with a little desi flavor where it belongs). Real numbers, named sources, and zero paid shilling. If a coin is trash, we say so. If regulation is confusing, we break it down line by line.</p>

      <li><strong>Exchanges &amp; How-to:</strong> INR deposits, P2P safety, tax filing steps</li>
    </ul>

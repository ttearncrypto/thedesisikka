# Structure planning

1. List the reader's real questions (People Also Ask, competitor H2s, forum threads in results).
2. Rank them by how much the reader cares. Give the top 1 or 2 the most words (often 30 to 40% of the article together).
3. Answer the main query in the first 100 words in plain sentences. No "The short answer" label; just answer.
4. Use a table only when comparing 3+ items across 2+ attributes. Otherwise prose.
5. FAQ only for questions not covered above, max 4, answers 1 to 3 sentences. Skip FAQ if everything is covered.
6. Vary H2 phrasing: some questions, some statements, some plain labels. Use H3 only where an H2 truly has sub parts.
7. Last section is useful content, not a summary. No "Conclusion" or "Bottom Line" heading.
8. Section lengths should differ visibly. If three sections in a row are within 20% of each other in word count, rebalance.

Sample outline shape for 1400 words (adapt, never copy):
- Intro answer (100 to 150)
- The question people care most about (350 to 450)
- Second key question (250)
- Practical part: how to do or check it (250)
- A misconception or competitor gap, with your view (150 to 200)
- 2 to 3 short FAQs (100 to 150)
- Sources list

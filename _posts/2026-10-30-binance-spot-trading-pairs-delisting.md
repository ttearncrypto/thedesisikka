Binance will remove and cease trading on 12 spot trading pairs starting 2026-10-30 03:00 UTC as part of its periodic review of listed pairs.

## Affected pairs

- AAVE/BTC
- ATOM/BTC
- CAKE/BTC
- DASH/BTC
- ETC/BTC
- ICP/BTC
- PENDLE/BTC
- RENDER/BTC
- TAO/BTC
- THETA/BTC
- XAUT/BTC
- XTZ/BTC

## What you need to know

- **Tokens remain available:** The delisting of a spot trading pair does not affect availability of the base or quote assets. Users can still trade them on other Binance spot pairs.
- **Trading bots:** Binance will terminate Spot Trading Bots services for these pairs at 2026-10-30 03:00 UTC. Users should update or cancel bots before then to avoid potential losses.
- **Review process:** Pairs are delisted periodically based on factors including poor liquidity and trading volume.

Binance advises users to review open orders and bot strategies for these pairs before the delisting takes effect.

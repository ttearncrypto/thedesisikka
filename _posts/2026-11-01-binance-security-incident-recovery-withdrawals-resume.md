Binance has announced the start of phased withdrawal resumption following its recent security incident. Withdrawals are set to resume in phase one on 2026-11-01 between 06:00 and 09:00 UTC, with deposits remaining open throughout.

## What’s reopening first

Phase one will cover withdrawals for select networks and assets, including:

- **Bitcoin (BTC):** BTC
- **Ethereum (ETH):** ETH, USDT, USDC
- **BNB Chain (BSC):** BNB, USDT, USDC
- **Tron (TRX):** TRX, USDT
- **Solana (SOL):** SOL, USDT, USDC
- **XRP:** XRP
- **Zcash (ZEC):** ZEC
- **Dogecoin (DOGE):** DOGE
- **TON:** USDT, GRAM
- **Polygon (MATIC):** POL, USDT, USDC
- **Avalanche C-Chain (AVAXC):** AVAX, USDT, USDC
- **Arbitrum:** ETH, USDT, USDC, ARB
- **Base:** ETH, USDC
- **Optimism (OP):** ETH, USDT, USDC
- **Sui (SUI):** SUI

Additional networks and assets will be added in later phases as checks are completed.

## Security and user protections

Binance says user funds remain safe and the exchange will absorb the financial impact of stolen funds. According to the update, Binance has covered approximately 95% of the impacted amount, up from 90% previously. All major assets including BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB, SOL, USDC, XRP, USD1, RLUSD, and ZEC are reported as fully backed 100%, verifiable via Binance Proof of Reserves.

Spot trading bots for the affected pairs will be terminated at the time of resumption where applicable, and users are advised to update or cancel bots before trading resumes for those pairs.

## What to expect

During the initial rollout, users may see temporary delays due to traffic volume. The exchange is taking a controlled, phased approach prioritizing stability and security checks before reopening more services. Binance says it will share further updates via its official X channel and publish a post-incident report once the investigation is complete.

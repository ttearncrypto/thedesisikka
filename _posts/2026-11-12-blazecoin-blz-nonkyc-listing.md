Blazecoin (BLZ) opened a single BLZ/USDT market on NonKYC.io at 11:15 UTC on November 12, 2026. Deposits and withdrawals are live, the minimum deposit is 1,000 BLZ, and the exchange credits deposits after 20 confirmations. The Blazecoin NonKYC listing is the coin's second live venue.

At 16:32 UTC, about five hours after the open, the pair had traded 399,180 BLZ for $467.74, according to NonKYC's market API. Last price: $0.00097962. The session high of $0.0018535 was more than double the low of $0.00087673.

NonKYC reports a market cap of $796,315 on 840.2 million circulating BLZ, under a cent a coin.

## What is Blazecoin (BLZ)?

Blazecoin is a scrypt proof-of-work coin that has run on its own chain since May 2014. It is a native coin with no ICO or token sale.

Proof of work is a consensus method where miners spend computing power to add blocks and earn coins. Scrypt is a memory-hard variant of that algorithm, the same family Litecoin uses.

The chain traded on Cryptopia from 2015 to 2018, then went quiet after that exchange collapsed in 2019. The original team is gone. In 2026 the current maintainers moved the node software to a Bitcoin Core 28 base, kept the original ledger, and added a per-block difficulty retarget.

The supply numbers as of November 12, 2026:

- Circulating supply: 840.2 million BLZ.
- Block reward: 25.8125 BLZ.
- Next halving: block 5,256,000.
- Premine: 20,650,000 BLZ in block 1, about 2.3% of total emission.

A premine is coins created before public mining starts. This one dates to 2014.

The chain also accepts post-quantum signatures. Post-quantum signatures are cryptographic signatures built to resist attacks from quantum computers. Since block 4,250,000, BLZ wallets can use ML-DSA-44, the NIST FIPS 204 standard, through a BQ address type. Legacy B addresses still work.

## Why does a 2014 coin need a second exchange?

Because the first one is small and the aggregators do not track it.

Blazecoin's CoinMarketCap page has existed since 2014 and is still marked untracked, an artifact of the Cryptopia closure. CoinGecko has been inactive on the coin since 2021.

The project says it asked CoinGecko to re-track BLZ after the second market opened. CoinMarketCap will be approached once the new market meets its trading-volume requirement. Two venues give those requests something to point at. Five hours of $467 volume does not give them much.

## How thin is the BLZ/USDT market?

Thin enough that a few hundred dollars counts as size. The whole pool holds about $719.50 across both sides, and the pair has done $467.74 in a session.

The spread sits at 0.499%, which is wide for a USDT pair on an exchange that runs automated market making. NonKYC's listing page says its pool places small orders to keep a pair active and warns not to expect high volume.

The first session high was $0.0018535, the low was $0.00087673, and the last trade came in near the low. A 111% range on day one is what a shallow book looks like when one order arrives.

The listing is real.

The order book is the weak part. Volume has to grow before a second venue changes anything for holders.

## What should you check before depositing BLZ?

Send from a V2 wallet, and send at least 1,000 BLZ in one transaction. Smaller deposits do not credit.

Wait for 20 confirmations, about 10 minutes at a 30-second block time. Do not point a mining pool payout at a NonKYC deposit address, because the exchange will not credit it.

Withdrawals go to legacy B addresses. Check the address format before you send, since a wrong route can take days to recover, if it recovers.

Use limit orders. NonKYC has market orders disabled on this pair, and the spread means a market fill would be expensive even if it were allowed.

Check the domain. NonKYC flagged a phishing site at nonkyc.at on October 25, 2026, and says nonkyc.io is its only official domain. Blazecoin's own warning is blunter: BLZ trades on KpowBIT and NonKYC, and anyone offering a listing, an allocation or an upgrade is running a scam.

Indian residents still owe the 30% flat tax on crypto gains and 1% TDS on transfers, whichever exchange they use.

## What happens next?

The first checkpoint is CoinGecko's re-tracking request. CoinMarketCap comes later, and it depends on volume the pair has not shown yet.

After that, watch the pool balance and the daily volume. If the pool stays near $700 and volume stays under $1,000 a day, BLZ has two markets and one small crowd. The second listing widens access. Buyers are a separate question.

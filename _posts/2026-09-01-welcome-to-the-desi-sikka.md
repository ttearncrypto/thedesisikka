Crypto news moves fast. Most of it reads like it was written for people who already know the jargon. The DESI Sikka flips that.

We're a new crypto news desk with one rule: crypto news, zero jargon. You get the story, the numbers, and the context. No glossary required.

## What The DESI Sikka covers

Six categories. That's the whole site.

**[Bitcoin]({{ '/category/bitcoin/' | relative_url }}).** Price moves, network upgrades, mining, and the macro story that moves BTC. When Bitcoin drops 12% in a day, you get the liquidation data, not the panic.

**[Ethereum]({{ '/category/ethereum/' | relative_url }}).** The merge aftermath, gas fees, layer-2 scaling, and what's actually happening on-chain. We explain why ETH matters for holders and builders.

**[Altcoins]({{ '/category/altcoins/' | relative_url }}).** Beyond the top two. Meme coins, new chains, token launches, and the ones that actually have working products. We separate signal from noise.

**[Regulation]({{ '/category/regulation/' | relative_url }}).** SEC enforcement, EU MiCA, and the rulebook of every jurisdiction that matters. When a government moves on crypto, we explain what it changes for you.

**[DeFi & Web3]({{ '/category/defi/' | relative_url }}).** Total value locked (TVL), yield farming, decentralized exchanges, and the protocols that handle real money. We explain the mechanics. Marketing needs no help from us.

**[Exchanges & How-to]({{ '/category/exchanges/' | relative_url }}).** Platform reviews, deposit methods, KYC processes, and the practical steps of actually using crypto. The stuff that doesn't make headlines but matters when you're setting up an account.

## Why we built this

Crypto has a language problem. Newcomers hit a wall of acronyms on day one. TVL, DEX, CEX, APY, impermanent loss. It's a barrier that keeps regular people out.

We think crypto news should work for everyone. If you're a veteran who wants fast, verified facts without fluff, we're for you. If you're new and trying to figure out what Bitcoin actually is, we're also for you.

We report for readers worldwide, across every timezone. The standard is the same everywhere: verified facts, plain English.

## What you'll get

Every article passes three checks before it ships. Fact-check: every number, date, and name has to trace to a source. Voice-check: it has to sound human, not like a press release. And the writing has to be clear enough that someone reading it for the first time can follow along.

No financial advice. We describe what happened and what to watch. You make your own calls.

The site is live. Bookmark The DESI Sikka and check back daily. New to crypto? Our [learn guides]({{ '/pages/learn/' | relative_url }}) explain the basics without the homework.

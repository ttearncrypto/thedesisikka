The DESI Sikka is a new global crypto news desk that launched on September 1, 2026 with one rule: crypto news, zero jargon. We cover bitcoin, ethereum and altcoin moves, DeFi and Web3 explainers, exchange guides, regulation and daily trading combos in plain English, with deep coverage of India. Every story runs through a fact-check, a voice-check, and a clarity pass before it ships.

## What The DESI Sikka covers

Seven sections. That's the whole site.

**[Bitcoin]({{ '/category/bitcoin/' | relative_url }}).** Price moves, network upgrades, mining, and the macro story that moves BTC. When Bitcoin drops 12% in a day, you get the liquidation data, not the panic.

**[Ethereum]({{ '/category/ethereum/' | relative_url }}).** Gas fees, layer-2 scaling, staking, and what's actually happening on-chain. We explain why ETH matters for holders and builders.

**[Altcoins]({{ '/category/altcoins/' | relative_url }}).** Beyond the top two. Meme coins, new chains, token launches, and the ones that actually have working products. We separate signal from noise.

**[Regulation]({{ '/category/regulation/' | relative_url }}).** SEC enforcement, EU MiCA, and the rulebook of every jurisdiction that matters. When a government moves on crypto, we explain what it changes for you.

**[DeFi & Web3]({{ '/category/defi/' | relative_url }}).** Total value locked (TVL), yield farming, decentralized exchanges, and the protocols that handle real money. We explain the mechanics. Marketing needs no help from us.

**[Exchanges & How-to]({{ '/category/exchanges/' | relative_url }}).** Platform reviews, deposit methods, KYC processes, and the practical steps of actually using crypto. The stuff that doesn't make headlines but matters when you're setting up an account.

**[Today's Combo]({{ '/category/todays-combo/' | relative_url }}).** One card every morning: the top story, a key price level, a tax note, and one airdrop worth your time. A complete briefing in under two minutes.

## Why we built this

Crypto has a language problem. Newcomers hit a wall of acronyms on day one: TVL, DEX, CEX, APY, impermanent loss. It's a barrier that keeps regular people out.

We think crypto news should work for everyone. If you're a veteran who wants fast, verified facts without fluff, we're for you. If you're new and trying to figure out what Bitcoin actually is, we're also for you.

We report for readers worldwide, across every timezone. The standard is the same everywhere: verified facts, plain English.

## How news gets made here

Every article passes three checks before it ships.

**Fact-check.** Every number, date, and name has to trace to a source. We link out to the original announcement, the on-chain data, or the official document. If we can't verify it, it doesn't run. When a figure came from a community trackers rather than an official statement, we say so out loud.

**Voice-check.** Copy has to read like a sharp human wrote it, not a press release machine. Short paragraphs, contractions, specific numbers, no hype. One desk, every timezone.

**Clarity pass.** The piece has to be followable by someone reading it for the first time. Jargon gets explained the first time it appears. Acronyms get spelled out.

## What we don't do

No financial advice. We describe what happened and what to watch. You make your own calls, ideally with a qualified advisor.

No paid coverage pretending to be news. Press releases from exchanges and protocols run in their own labeled section, kept separate from reporting. If we hold a coin, we disclose it. If we correct a mistake, it goes in the [corrections register]({{ '/corrections/' | relative_url }}).

Ledn confirmed on October 20 that loans backed by Tether Gold start rolling out in November 2026. You'll pledge tokenized gold, borrow USDT or USAT against it, and hold on to the gold exposure as long as the loan stays healthy.

That is the first time a crypto lender has put tokenized bullion up as collateral on a retail product. Gold-backed lending has belonged to central banks, bullion dealers and large banks. Tokenizing the bar is what moves it onto a crypto app.

## What Ledn actually confirmed

Ledn's October 20 company post was mostly a rebrand, but it carried a short and concrete roadmap for November 2026. Three products are coming: custody for assets you hold with Ledn, new ways to move money between fiat and hard assets, and loans backed by tokenized gold.

The track record behind that list is worth reading. Ledn says it has funded more than $11 billion in loans since 2018. It says collateral is held in custody and never lent out to earn interest. It says it was the first in digital asset lending to publish an independent Proof of Reserves attestation, and that its monthly Open Book Report has its metrics independently reported by The Network Firm LLP. It is SOC 2 Type 2 certified.

Then there's the bond. In February 2026, Ledn completed the first asset-backed securities transaction backed by a portfolio of bitcoin-backed loans. S&P Global Ratings gave the senior notes a BBB- investment grade rating, the highest any bitcoin instrument has received. That deal was also the first to include loans to clients in 30 different countries without treating them as different risks.

Tether isn't a stranger here. The stablecoin issuer made a strategic investment in Ledn in November 2025.

## How the XAUT loan works

The mechanics follow Ledn's bitcoin lending book:

- You deposit XAUT as collateral and receive a loan in USDT or USAT.
- The collateral is held one-for-one, separate from Ledn's own funds, and it isn't rehypothecated.
- There are no monthly payments, and you can repay whenever you want.
- Loan-to-value ratios and interest rates haven't been published yet.

Gold moves, so the collateral gets liquidated if the price falls below the threshold. That's the risk sitting under the product, and it's the same risk a bitcoin-backed borrower carries.

Two jurisdictions are excluded at launch: Canada and the EU. Ledn's Cayman entity is registered as a virtual asset service provider with the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority, and its Spanish arm is still working through a MiCA licence with the CNMV.

## How much gold Tether actually holds

This is where the numbers get quoted badly, so here's the clean version.

Jefferies estimated in February that Tether held 148 tonnes of physical gold as of January 31, 2026, worth roughly $23 billion. On that estimate Tether sits in the world's top 30 gold holders, ahead of Australia, the UAE, Qatar, South Korea and Greece.

Tether's own quarterly report told a similar story. For the first quarter of 2026, gold backing USDT reserves rose 6 tonnes to 132 tonnes, and the gold backing XAUT rose 6 tonnes from December to 22.2 tonnes. Combined, that's about 154 tonnes across both products. The same report valued the gold inside USDT reserves at $19.8 billion, with $117 billion in US Treasury bills, gold at 10% of reserves, and $7 billion in bitcoin. Tether bought another 14 tonnes in the second quarter.

Worth keeping straight: the $189.5 billion figure in Tether's first-quarter report is the value of USDT tokens in circulation, not gold. The gold number is $23 billion.

CEO Paolo Ardoino told Reuters in January that Tether aims to put 10% to 15% of its $20 billion investment portfolio into physical gold. The metal is stored in Swiss vaults, and Ardoino described one of the facilities to Bloomberg as a "James Bond kind of place".

## Where XAUT trades

XAUT sat near $4,100 on October 21 and 22, down about 5.4% over 30 days. Market cap is close to $3.3 billion, 24-hour volume runs between $228 million and $316 million depending on the tracker, and circulating supply is about 809,000 tokens. The all-time high is $5,504.62.

The reserve growth underneath it has been fast. Tether's data showed 707,747.14 fine troy ounces backing XAUT at March 31, up from 520,089.35 at the end of 2025, a 36% jump in three months. Market value of those reserves went from roughly $2.25 billion to more than $3.3 billion over the same stretch, with 559,598.64 tokens sold in the quarter.

The wider market for tokenized gold passed $5.21 billion in early October, and XAUT holds roughly 45% of it. Holder wallets grew to 143,973 by October 2026, up from 12,233 a year earlier.

## What to watch

- **Whether the loans actually ship in November.** Ledn said November 2026. Platform products slip.
- **The rate card.** Without published loan-to-value ratios and rates, you can't compare this with selling gold and buying back, or with borrowing against bitcoin.
- **Physical redemption.** CoinStats reported in October that Tether's minimum physical redemption is 50 XAUT, with roughly 430 XAUT of bullion delivered in Switzerland.
- **Competitors.** Paxos Gold has no comparable lending integration right now. It won't stay that way if this works.

## Bottom line

Ledn has confirmed that XAUT-backed loans start arriving in November 2026, with USDT and USAT loans against tokenized gold that stays in custody and never gets lent out. The company's record, an $11 billion-plus lending book and an S&P-rated bond, is stronger than the crypto lending boom it grew out of.

Gold at $4,100 is 26% below its all-time high, and the collateral gets liquidated if it falls further. Whether this product matters depends on rates that haven't been published yet, and on whether November holds.

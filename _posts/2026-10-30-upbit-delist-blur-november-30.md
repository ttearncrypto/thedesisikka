Upbit Indonesia will stop BLUR/BTC trading at 13:00 WIB on November 30, 2026. The notice follows an investment warning the exchange placed on Blur on October 13, and a second review that found the issues behind it were not fixed.

Withdrawals remain open for 30 days after the delisting, through December 29, 2026. Deposits are already closed.

## What did Upbit say about Blur?

Upbit said it reviewed Blur for project sustainability and business viability. The review found several weak points and a risk of user harm, which led to the October 13 warning.

After another review, the exchange said the grounds for that warning had not been resolved. That moved BLUR into the delisting process under Upbit's digital asset policy.

An investment warning is a risk label Upbit places on a coin it thinks could hurt users. It is not a final delisting notice on its own. The exchange can lift the label if a project fixes the problem, and it can also use the label as the first step toward removal. For BLUR, the second path is the one that played out.

## What happens to orders and deposits?

The clock runs in two stages.

Trading stops at 13:00 WIB on November 30, 2026, which is 06:00 UTC. Every open buy and sell order on BLUR/BTC gets canceled when trading support ends. Do not count on a resting order to fill after that cut-off.

Withdrawals keep working until December 29, 2026. That gives holders 30 days after the delisting date to move coins off the exchange.

Deposits are a different story. Upbit said it will not support BLUR deposits once the notice is published. A transfer sent after that point may not credit. If coins land after the withdrawal window closes, Upbit warns the return process can take a long time for technical reasons.

Airdrops, wallet upgrades, and hard forks for BLUR will not be supported after the notice either.

## Why is Blur being delisted?

The exchange decision lands on top of a project that is already winding down.

Blur announced on October 12 that it will retire the blur.io frontend on November 12, 2026. The same date marks the end of new loan matching on Blend, its NFT lending product. The smart contracts stay on Ethereum, and the token keeps its governance and fee-switch role, but the main interface most users know is going away.

The numbers behind that retreat are brutal. Blur's monthly NFT trading volume peaked at about $1.43 billion in March 2023, according to DefiLlama data cited by CoinDesk. By March 2026 it had fallen below $7 million, a decline of more than 99.5%.

Fees followed the same path. BeInCrypto reported that Blur collected $25.58 million in fees in the first quarter of 2023 and just $0.51 in the third quarter of 2026.

The token has tracked the business. BLUR hit an all-time high of $5.02 in February 2023, per DefiLlama. It has lost more than 99% since then and traded near $0.0074 on October 30. Market cap sits around $21.6 million.

Blur is tied to Tieshun Roquerre, known as Pacman, who also founded Blast. Blast announced its own shutdown earlier in October, which put two of his projects in retreat within days of each other.

## What should BLUR holders do before November 30?

First, check where the coins sit. This notice covers Upbit Indonesia and the BLUR/BTC pair. If your BLUR is on another venue, that venue has its own schedule, so read its notice instead of assuming this one applies.

If you use Upbit Indonesia, decide before 13:00 WIB on November 30 whether to sell into the market or withdraw. Open orders will be wiped at the cut-off, so a limit order is not a plan.

Withdrawals stay open until December 29, which gives you room to move after trading ends. Check the network shown in the app before sending, because a transfer on the wrong chain can fail to credit. Send a small test amount first if you are moving a large balance.

The token is not disappearing. BLUR still trades on other exchanges and lives on Ethereum. What ends is this exchange's support for the pair.

## Who else is dropping BLUR?

South Korean venues moved on the same day. Bithumb said it will end BLUR and Core (CORE) trading at 15:00 KST on November 30, with withdrawals through December 30. That 15:00 KST cut-off is 13:00 WIB, the same moment as Upbit Indonesia's.

Upbit's Korean platform also set a November 30 BLUR delisting, with its own withdrawal deadline. Korean users there face the same two-step choice: trade before the cut-off, then withdraw before that venue's window closes.

## Bottom line

Upbit Indonesia closes BLUR/BTC at 13:00 WIB on November 30, 2026, and withdrawals end December 29. The delisting follows a warning the project could not clear, and it arrives two weeks after Blur's own frontend shutdown.

If you hold BLUR on the exchange, put both dates in a calendar now. The trading deadline is the one that decides whether you sell or withdraw, and the withdrawal deadline is the one that decides whether you keep access to the coins.

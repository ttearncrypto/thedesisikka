Soqucoin (SOQ) started trading on [NonKYC.io](https://nonkyc.io/?ref=657ee52b7dcff9a0fc0cfa38) at 18:30 UTC on October 18, 2026, opening in two markets: SOQ/USDT and SOQ/BTC. Deposits and withdrawals are live, the minimum deposit is 5,000 SOQ, and it is the post-quantum Scrypt coin's second exchange listing, five days after its mainnet launch.

## What is Soqucoin?

Soqucoin is a Scrypt proof-of-work cryptocurrency that signs every transaction with ML-DSA-44, a post-quantum digital signature standard finalized by NIST. Post-quantum cryptography means the math behind transaction signatures is designed to resist attacks from quantum computers, which would break the elliptic-curve signatures used by Bitcoin and most other chains.

The chain launched mainnet on October 13 at 15:00 UTC with no premine. SOQ is merge-mined with Litecoin through auxiliary proof-of-work (AuxPoW), which lets any Scrypt miner producing Litecoin or Dogecoin blocks mine SOQ at the same time, with no extra energy cost. The project's own pool, SOQUPOOL 2.0, hit 25 TH/s within two days of opening to all Scrypt miners on October 15. It charges a flat 1.69% fee and pays out every four hours.

Soqucoin is built by Soqucoin Labs Inc., registered in Wyoming, and its code has been audited by Halborn Security. A second Halborn phase covering the project's planned features is still pending.

## What are the NonKYC.io listing details?

Markets opened October 18 at 18:30 UTC. The live pairs:

- [SOQ/USDT market on NonKYC.io](https://nonkyc.io/market/SOQ_USDT)
- [SOQ/BTC market on NonKYC.io](https://nonkyc.io/market/SOQ_BTC)
- [SOQ/USDT liquidity pool](https://nonkyc.io/pool/SOQ_USDT)
- [SOQ/BTC liquidity pool](https://nonkyc.io/pool/SOQ_BTC)

NonKYC runs liquidity pools on both pairs, about $909 each for roughly $1,817 combined as of October 19. The exchange's [SOQ asset page](https://nonkyc.io/asset/SOQ) shows deposits and withdrawals active and publishes proof of reserve. The 5,000 SOQ minimum deposit means dust transfers will not credit.

First-session volume ran about $912 across both markets, with $775 on SOQ/USDT and $138 on SOQ/BTC, per NonKYC's asset page. Early SOQ/USDT prints sat near $0.000149, according to aggregator data from Coinsprey. Thin, which is what day one usually looks like.

## Why does the post-quantum angle matter?

Every SOQ transaction is signed with CRYSTALS-Dilithium, the algorithm NIST standardized as ML-DSA-44. It has been live on the network since the genesis block, and Halborn Security audited the code. The firm also published a case study calling Soqucoin the first native post-quantum Scrypt proof-of-work blockchain.

The pitch: quantum computers, if they scale, could derive private keys from the public keys that elliptic-curve signatures expose. Lattice-based schemes like Dilithium are built to resist that.

## What should traders watch next?

Liquidity is the immediate question. About $1,817 in the pools is thin even by new-listing standards, and SOQ's other venue, SafeTrade, has carried most of the trading volume since October 13. Whether NonKYC's order books deepen depends partly on the migration community: roughly 70.33 billion SOQ were allocated to pSOQ burners in the September 29 to October 5 window, and those allocations were committed to mainnet block 1.

The project has said applications are in at CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko, and a third centralized exchange is in discussions. NonKYC's [full listing announcement](https://nonkyc.io/post/new_listing_soqucoin_soq) has the complete asset details.

## Bottom line

SOQ now trades against both USDT and BTC on a no-KYC exchange, five days into mainnet. The post-quantum signatures and Litecoin merge-mining are real and audited. The order books are thin. Watch whether pool liquidity and volume grow past the first week before drawing conclusions about demand.

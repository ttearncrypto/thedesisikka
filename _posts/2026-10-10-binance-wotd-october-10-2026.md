## What is Binance Word of the Day?

Binance Word of the Day, shortened to WOTD, is a daily word game inside the Binance app under Gifts and Campaigns. It plays like Wordle: guess a hidden crypto-related word, and after each try the app colors the tiles so you know which letters are right.

The color system is simple. Green means the letter is correct and in the right spot. Yellow means the letter is in the word but in the wrong position. Black or grey means it is not in the word at all. Use that feedback to shrink the options instead of guessing blind.

This week's theme points at the basics of staying safe on an exchange: turn on two-step verification, watch your login alerts, and never share a password or PIN. Words like VERIFY, SHIELD and FREEZE are the vocabulary to expect.

## How do I play Binance Word of the Day?

Open the official Binance app and head to the campaign. From the bottom menu tap **More**, then open **Gifts and Campaigns**, then pick **Word of the Day**. You can also jump straight to the Word of the Day page on the [official Binance site](https://www.binance.com). Enter your guesses and read the colors after each attempt. Most players finish a day's puzzle in under two minutes.

You can earn extra attempts by sharing the official campaign link. When someone else opens it, you typically get one more guess. It is a small referral bump, but it doubles your daily attempts and rescues a stuck puzzle.

## How much can you earn from Binance WOTD?

Rewards change by round, so treat any number you read elsewhere with suspicion. Some tracker sites still print a 500,000 BNB or Binance Points figure, and that copy comes from 2021-era campaigns. The current numbers are smaller. The October 5 to 11 cycle follows the recent structure: a 10,000 USDC pool for players who log at least five correct words, plus $50 of equivalent trading volume. Earlier rounds split their pool into a 3,000 USDC tier for five correct answers and a 7,000 USDC tier that also required $50 in non-zero fee trading on Spot, Convert, TradFi, bStocks, or Futures, capped at 5 USDC per user.

Binance can change the pool, the eligibility rules, or the prize vouchers at any time without notice. Binance has its own reporting rules for some regions, and crypto rewards are taxable in many places. Check the campaign page inside the app for today's real numbers before you put time in. The safest habit is to treat every third-party word list, including this one, as a starting point, and verify in-app.

## Why do some users see different Binance WOTD answers?

Binance hands out different word lengths, and sometimes different words, to different accounts even under the same daily theme. That is deliberate. If everyone got one shared answer, the challenge would collapse on day one. Count your tiles, pick the matching group, and ignore anyone who claims a single universal answer.

If the answers here look like a mismatch, log in, open the puzzle, and read your actual grid. Themes change weekly and words rotate, so yesterday's list will not save you tomorrow.

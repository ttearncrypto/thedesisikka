Upbit Indonesia opened trading for Collector Crypt (CARDS) and Dolphin (POD) at 12:00 WIB on November 9, 2026. That is 05:00 UTC. The Upbit Indonesia listing covers four pairs: CARDS/BTC, CARDS/USDT, POD/BTC and POD/USDT. Deposits went live 30 minutes after the exchange published the notice.

Both CARDS and POD trade against BTC and USDT. Neither has a rupiah pair, so Indonesian users need BTC or USDT in their account to trade either token.

## What pairs does the Upbit Indonesia listing add?

| Asset | Pairs | Trading start | Deposits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collector Crypt (CARDS) | CARDS/BTC, CARDS/USDT | 12:00 WIB, November 9, 2026 | 30 minutes after notice |
| Dolphin (POD) | POD/BTC, POD/USDT | 12:00 WIB, November 9, 2026 | 30 minutes after notice |

If the pairs do not show up on the web terminal, refresh with F5. On the mobile app, update the listing data under Settings > Display Settings > Exchange > Update Listing Data.

## Why list these two now?

Upbit's Korean venue already did. It added POD on October 2, 2026, and CARDS on November 5, 2026, both with KRW, BTC and USDT pairs. The Indonesian arm is extending access to local users. Upbit Korea got there first.

That changes what the news means. A first listing on a major exchange can move a token because it opens new demand from traders who could not reach the asset before. A second venue in the same exchange group mostly widens the door for one country's traders. Useful, but smaller.

The Indonesian notice also skips the won. Korean traders get a direct fiat pair. Indonesian traders get BTC and USDT only.

## What is Collector Crypt (CARDS)?

Collector Crypt is a Solana-based marketplace that turns physical trading cards and other collectibles into NFTs. Each NFT represents a graded card held in an insured storage facility, and the holder can trade it or burn it to have the physical card shipped.

The platform runs a gacha machine, where users pay a set amount for a random collectible, plus a marketplace where holders trade the NFTs and redeem them for physical cards. CARDS is the ecosystem token for peer-to-peer transactions and rewards.

The numbers as of November 9, 2026:

- Price: $0.219, down 1.65% in 24 hours.
- Market cap: $195.1 million.
- Fully diluted valuation: $431.8 million.
- Circulating supply: 890.9 million of a 2 billion cap.
- 24-hour volume: $8 million.
- All-time high: $0.3825 on October 21, 2026. The token trades about 43% below that.

That gap between market cap and FDV is the part to watch. Less than half the supply circulates.

The locked insider allocation releases monthly through July 29, 2027, so new tokens keep reaching the market. A listing can absorb some of that. It does not cancel it.

## What is Dolphin (POD)?

Dolphin Network is an AI DePIN project. It fine-tunes open AI models and spreads inference work across idle GPUs. The network uses a Peer-to-Pool design: GPUs running the same model join one pool, and requests go to whichever node is free.

Node operators supply the GPUs. Validators check that other nodes run the assigned model correctly. POD pays for staking and bonding. The project also runs a Web Chat and a Telegram bot, and it built the Dolphin Mistral 24B Venice Edition with Venice.

POD runs on Base. Upbit's Korean notice lists the contract as 0xeD664536023d8E4b1640C394777D34aBAFF1dF8F.

The numbers as of November 9, 2026:

- Price: $0.450, down 4.4% in 24 hours.
- Market cap: $37.7 million.
- Fully diluted valuation: $225.2 million.
- Circulating supply: 83.7 million of a 500 million cap.
- 24-hour volume: $1.74 million.
- All-time high: $0.9545 on October 4, 2026. The token trades about 53% below that.

Dolphin says 100% of inference revenue goes to buying POD on the open market, which ties the token to paying demand rather than node emissions. That claim is the core of its value case. It also means the token depends on customers, not just node rewards.

## What should traders check before sending an order?

First, the network. Upbit's Korean notices allow CARDS deposits on Solana only and POD deposits on Base only, so a transfer sent on the wrong chain may not credit at all. The Indonesian app should show the supported network. Confirm it before you send, because a wrong-network deposit can take a long time to recover, if it recovers at all.

Second, the opening rules. Upbit Korea limited buys for about five minutes, blocked sells more than 10% below the prior close, and allowed only limit orders for roughly two hours after a listing. The Indonesian notice does not print the same schedule, so read the app before you size a trade.

Third, the pair. There is no CARDS/IDR or POD/IDR market. You need BTC or USDT to trade either token.

## What happens next?

CARDS gets another monthly release from its locked insider allocation on December 29, 2026. That adds supply regardless of how the new pairs trade.

For POD, watch revenue. If inference demand grows, the buyback story has something behind it. If it does not, a $225.2 million FDV sits far above a $37.7 million market cap with no earnings to close the gap.

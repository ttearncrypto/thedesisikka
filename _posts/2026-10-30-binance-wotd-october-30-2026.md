## What is Binance Word of the Day?

Binance Word of the Day, or WOTD, is a daily word game inside the Binance app under Gifts and Campaigns. It works like Wordle: guess a hidden crypto-related word, and the app colors the tiles after each attempt.

Green means the letter is correct and in the right spot. Yellow means the letter is in the word but in the wrong position. Black or grey means the letter is not in the word at all. Use each result to rule letters in or out.

## How do I play Binance Word of the Day?

Open the official Binance app, not a third-party site. Tap **More** in the bottom menu, open **Gifts and Campaigns**, then choose **Word of the Day**. The same game is linked from the [official Binance site](https://www.binance.com).

You can play up to two games a day in this cycle. The second opens when you tap **Get A New WOTD**, share the link, and get a logged-in user to click it. That doubles your daily attempts and matters more today than it did on day one.

## How much can you earn from Binance WOTD?

Binance lists 14 BNB for this Start with Binance AI Pro cycle.

The first pool is 4 BNB, shared by users who submit at least five correct answers. Your slice depends on how many right answers you have against the total from all eligible players.

The second pool is 10 BNB, for users who hit five correct answers and also finish a trading task. The task is either $50 in cumulative non-zero-fee trades on Spot, Convert, TradFi, bStocks, or Futures, or $30 in NEWT through Spot or Futures. That pool caps each user at 0.01 BNB.

Rewards are scheduled for the Rewards Hub by November 15, 2026, at 23:59 UTC. Binance says the activity is not available to users in the EEA.

Posts still circulate with a 500,000 BNB figure. That is stale copy from an old campaign, not the current pool.

Binance can change the pool, the eligibility rules, or the vouchers at any time without notice. Crypto rewards are taxable in many places, including India, where gains face a 30% flat tax and transfers carry a 1% TDS. Confirm today's numbers on the campaign page in the app.

## Why do some users see different Binance WOTD answers?

Binance assigns different word lengths, and sometimes different words, to different accounts under the same daily theme. One shared answer would collapse the puzzle on day one.

Count your tiles, pick the matching group, and ignore claims of a single universal answer. Themes change weekly and words rotate, so today's list will not help you tomorrow.

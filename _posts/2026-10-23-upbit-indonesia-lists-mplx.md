Upbit Indonesia opened Metaplex (MPLX) trading against BTC and USDT at 12:00 WIB on October 22, 2026. The notice said deposits would open 30 minutes after it was published. Indonesian users now have two crypto-quoted pairs, and there is no rupiah market.

MPLX is an SPL token on Solana, so the first practical check before moving coins is the network shown in the app. A transfer on the wrong chain can fail to credit.

## What did Upbit Indonesia list?

Upbit Indonesia added MPLX to its BTC and USDT markets. Trading started at 12:00 WIB, which is 05:00 UTC. The exchange did not open an MPLX/IDR pair.

An IDR pair matters for local users because it removes a conversion step. With BTC and USDT only, a rupiah holder has to buy bitcoin or tether first, then buy MPLX. That adds two spreads and a second order book to the trade.

Upbit Indonesia is licensed and supervised by OJK, the country's financial services authority.

If the pair does not appear in the app, refresh the listing data. On mobile: Settings > Display Settings > Exchange > Update Listing Data.

## What is Metaplex (MPLX)?

Metaplex is a digital asset infrastructure protocol for Solana and SVM-based networks. It sets the rules for how tokens and NFTs carry metadata such as name, image, collection, creator, and royalties, so wallets, marketplaces, games, and apps can read the same asset data.

The stack covers Token Metadata and Metaplex Core for asset standards, Candy Machine and Bubblegum V2 for collection mints and compressed NFTs, and Genesis and the Metaplex App for token launches and trading. Metaplex says its infrastructure has powered more than 1 billion transactions and created more than 1 billion digital assets.

MPLX is the protocol's governance and utility token. Holders use it in Metaplex DAO governance, and 50% of protocol fees are converted to MPLX and sent to the DAO treasury, according to Metaplex's fee documentation. The other half funds the Metaplex Foundation.

The DAO held 314 million MPLX, or 31.4% of total supply, as of October 14, plus 21.7 million MPLX in protocol-owned liquidity. In September, the DAO bought back 3.0 million MPLX after the protocol generated $196,000 in revenue. August was stronger at $226,000 and a 7.1 million token buyback.

Metaplex has also moved into agent infrastructure. Its Agent Registry gives AI agents an on-chain identity tied to an MPL Core asset, and Agent Tokens let a registered agent launch a token through Genesis to fund itself. The Agent Tokens launch in April 2026 came with six integration partners and $50,000 in MPLX rewards from the DAO.

## Why did MPLX spike before the Indonesian listing?

Before the Indonesian listing, the bigger price move came from Korea. Upbit's Korean exchange opened MPLX/KRW trading at 18:30 KST on October 20. MPLX jumped more than 110% to about $0.11 in the first minutes, then pulled back. By October 23 it traded near $0.06, according to Coinbase price data.

That pattern is common for new Upbit fiat pairs. A won market gives Korean retail a direct route into a token that previously required dollar or crypto pairs. The first prints are thin, and the price often cools once the initial orders clear.

The Indonesian listing is a smaller access change. MPLX already traded against BTC and USDT on Upbit's Korean venue since July 3, 2026. Indonesia adds a new set of users and a new order book, but the same two quote assets.

## What should traders watch after the listing?

Liquidity is the first thing to check. A new pair can show a wide spread and thin depth for days. Compare the MPLX/BTC and MPLX/USDT books before sizing an order, and remember that the BTC pair adds a bitcoin conversion on top.

Watch whether Upbit Indonesia adds an MPLX/IDR market. That would be the bigger change for local users because it removes the BTC or USDT leg.

On the protocol side, the next data point is the October revenue and buyback update, usually published in the middle of the following month. September revenue fell to $196,000 from $226,000 in August, and the monthly buyback dropped to 3.0 million MPLX from 7.1 million. A second month of lower fees would matter more than a listing headline.

Also track Agent Tokens and MPL-3643, the permissioned token standard Metaplex unveiled on September 29 for real-world assets and tokenized securities. Both are attempts to widen the protocol's revenue base beyond NFT and token metadata fees.

## Bottom line

MPLX now trades against BTC and USDT on Upbit Indonesia, with no rupiah pair. The token has real fee revenue, a DAO treasury that holds roughly a third of supply, and a fresh Korean listing pop in its recent history. The Indonesian listing is live, but the next move depends on liquidity in the two new books and whether protocol fees stop sliding.

The Binance NUUSDT contract opens at 09:30 UTC on November 12, 2026. The FRHCUSDT perpetual follows 15 minutes later. Both settle in USDT, track Nu Holdings (NYSE: NU) and Freedom Holding (Nasdaq: FRHC), and offer up to 20x.

Neither expires.

The two listings extend Binance's TradFi perpetual line, which already carries stock contracts like Tesla and Intel plus index ETFs. This pair adds a Brazilian digital bank and a Kazakhstan-focused financial group to a market that trades through weekends.

## How does a stock perpetual trade when the stock market is shut?

A perpetual futures contract is a derivative that tracks an asset's price with no expiry date. Traders post margin, pay or receive a funding rate, and the position stays open until they close it. Nobody involved owns the underlying stock, so there are no dividends and no shareholder votes.

Binance prices its TradFi contracts off a real-time Price Index during the underlying market's trading hours. Outside those hours it uses an exponentially weighted moving average, or EWMA, of recent prices. An EWMA is a running average that gives more weight to newer data.

The design matters for a product trading 24/7. On a Sunday, there is no fresh NYSE or Nasdaq print, so the contract tracks a smoothed version of Friday's level. A weekend move in NUUSDT is a bet on Monday's open. The cash market is shut.

Both contracts also skip Binance rule 8.1 on funding intervals. Under that rule, Binance normally moves funding from every eight hours to every hour once the rate hits the cap or floor. These two keep the eight-hour schedule until Binance announces a change.

## What do Nu Holdings and Freedom Holding actually do?

Nu Holdings is the parent of Nubank, the Brazilian digital bank. It serves more than 100 million customers across Brazil, Mexico and Colombia, and Brazil supplies more than 90% of its revenue. The stock closed at $12.32 on November 11, 2026, for a market cap of $59.51 billion, according to stockanalysis.com.

Freedom Holding is a Nasdaq-listed financial group with brokerage, banking and insurance operations in 24 countries, with a heavy presence in Kazakhstan. CEO Timur Turlov runs it. The stock traded at $163.21 at 11:10 a.m. ET on November 12, for a market cap of $10.41 billion.

Nu is the bigger company. Its stock is also the more liquid one.

Nu traded 58.3 million shares on November 11. Freedom Holding had traded about 20,300 shares by late morning on November 12. That is a thin cash market. A thinner underlying market makes a perp's index easier to move, which matters more on FRHC than on NU.

## What does 20x with a 2% funding cap mean?

At 20x, a 5% move against a fully margined position wipes out the margin before fees. The liquidation math is the same as any crypto perp. The stock label does not soften it.

The funding cap is the part traders underestimate. Two percent per eight-hour settlement works out to 6% a day if the rate sits at the ceiling. Most of the time funding stays far below that.

In a one-sided market, though, a new contract can run to the cap and stay there. These two contracts will not shorten their interval to cool off. The $5 minimum notional lets small accounts in. Funding applies to the notional value of the position, so a $100 position at 20x pays the same rate as a $2,000 position at 1x.

## What should traders watch on launch day?

Nu Holdings reports third-quarter 2026 results after the US close on November 12. The earnings call is scheduled for 5 p.m. ET, or 22:00 UTC, about 12.5 hours after NUUSDT opens. That puts the contract's first session around an event that can move the stock sharply.

Watch the funding rate through the first eight hours. A new perp with demand on one side can swing to the cap fast, and this one will not shorten its interval in response. Watch the gap between the perp and the $12.32 cash close too. If NUUSDT trades well above that level before the results land, the premium is positioning, not news.

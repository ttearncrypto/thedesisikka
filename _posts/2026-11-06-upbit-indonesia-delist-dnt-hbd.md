Upbit Indonesia will stop DNT/BTC and HBD/BTC trading at 13:00 WIB on December 7, 2026, which is 06:00 UTC. The exchange said the investment warnings it placed on district0x (DNT) and Hive Dollar (HBD) on October 16 have not been resolved.

Withdrawals stay open for 30 days after the delisting, until January 5, 2027. Deposits for both assets are already closed.

## What is the Upbit Indonesia delisting schedule?

| Asset | Pair | Trading stops | Withdrawals end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| district0x (DNT) | DNT/BTC | 13:00 WIB, December 7, 2026 | January 5, 2027 |
| Hive Dollar (HBD) | HBD/BTC | 13:00 WIB, December 7, 2026 | January 5, 2027 |

All pending buy and sell orders get canceled when trading support ends. Airdrops, wallet upgrades, and hard forks for either asset will not be supported after the notice.

Deposits stopped on October 16, when Upbit put both assets on its Investment Warning list. Anything sent after that point may not credit, and Upbit warns that returning mistaken transfers can take a long time.

## Why is Upbit delisting DNT and HBD?

Upbit reviewed each asset against its Digital Asset Trading Support Best Practices, then ran a second review after the October warning.

For DNT, the exchange looked at the business behind the token, its sustainability and progress, its use cases, its purpose or function, and its aggregate market cap across domestic and international venues. Upbit said it found several shortcomings and a risk of user harm.

For HBD, the review covered aggregate market cap, changes in the number of on-chain holders, trading volume, liquidity, and whether other venues still support the asset. Upbit said it found shortcomings in HBD's trading and adoption.

An Investment Warning is a risk label. It is not a final delisting notice on its own. Upbit can lift it if a project fixes the issue, or use it as the first step toward removal. For both assets, the second path played out.

Upbit Indonesia ran the same process on [Blur (BLUR)]({{ '/news/upbit-delist-blur-november-30/' | relative_url }}), which loses its BLUR/BTC pair on November 30, 2026.

## What is district0x (DNT)?

district0x is an Ethereum-based network of decentralized marketplaces and communities called districts. The districts run on d0xINFRA, an open-source framework of Ethereum smart contracts and front-end libraries.

DNT is the network's staking and governance token. Holders stake DNT to a district and get voting rights over that district's branding and fees. The token launched in 2017.

DNT trades at $0.00813, according to CoinGecko data on November 6, 2026. Market cap is about $8.13 million, and 24-hour volume is near $99,399. The token is down 98.3% from its April 2021 high of $0.48337.

On Upbit, DNT/BTC last traded at 0.00000011 BTC, about $0.0083, with 24-hour volume of 10.58 million DNT.

## What is Hive Dollar (HBD)?

HBD is the native stablecoin on the Hive blockchain. It is designed to hold $1 through two decentralized conversion operations between HIVE and HBD. The conversion price uses a 3.5-day median to blunt manipulation.

HBD also has a savings feature that pays variable interest set by Hive witnesses. The chain's safety rules stop new HBD creation for rewards and conversions when HBD debt reaches 20% of the combined HIVE and HBD value. At 30%, a haircut rule converts HBD to less than $1 of HIVE.

The Hive blockchain reported 31.94 million HBD in circulation on November 6, 2026.

HBD is not trading at $1 on Upbit. The HBD/BTC pair last traded at 0.00002431 BTC on November 6, about $1.83 at a BTC price of $75,382. That is an 83% premium to the peg.

The premium showed up after Upbit froze deposits on October 16. The pair hit a 52-week high of 0.000055 BTC on October 17, the day after the warning. A thin book and trapped supply can push a stablecoin far from its target. Read the Upbit quote as a venue price.

## What should DNT and HBD holders do before December 7?

Decide before 13:00 WIB on December 7 whether to sell or withdraw. Open orders are wiped at the cut-off, so a resting limit order is not a plan.

If you want to keep the asset, start the withdrawal before the deadline. Upbit supports withdrawals until January 5, 2027. Check the network in the app before sending, and test with a small amount first.

DNT still trades on other exchanges and lives on Ethereum. HBD remains a Hive native asset. What ends is Upbit Indonesia's support for these two BTC pairs.

If you hold either asset on another venue, read that venue's notice. This schedule applies to Upbit Indonesia.

## The dates that matter

Upbit Indonesia closes DNT/BTC and HBD/BTC at 13:00 WIB on December 7, 2026. Withdrawals run until January 5, 2027. Both delistings follow warnings the projects could not clear.

DNT is a 2017 Ethereum governance token trading near $0.008. HBD is Hive's stablecoin, quoted at a steep premium on the exchange that is about to drop it. If you hold either one there, put both dates in a calendar and decide before the cut-off.

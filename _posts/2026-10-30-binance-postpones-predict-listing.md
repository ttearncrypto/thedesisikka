Binance postponed the Predict (PREDICT) spot listing on October 30, 2026, hours before PREDICT/USDT, PREDICT/USDC and PREDICT/TRY were due to open at 10:00 UTC. The exchange gave no reason and set no new date. Predict.fun then delayed its token generation event and Season 1 airdrop claim.

The notice landed about nine minutes after Binance's first public statement on a $6.3 billion security incident. Binance has not said the two events are connected.

## What did Binance postpone?

Binance announced the listing on October 29 with a Seed Tag attached. The plan:

| Detail | Info |
| --- | --- |
| Token | Predict (PREDICT) |
| Pairs | PREDICT/USDT, PREDICT/USDC, PREDICT/TRY |
| Scheduled open | October 30, 2026, 10:00 UTC |
| Status | Postponed until further notice |
| Seed Tag | Applied |
| Chain | BNB Smart Chain |
| Contract | 0x8888cef3923385c7f90ade66d09f41fb16668888 |
| Listing fee | 0 BNB |

The TRY pair was limited to users with a verified Binance TR account. Withdrawals were scheduled for October 31 at 10:00 UTC.

Binance Wallet had also planned to open PREDICT trading on Binance Alpha at 09:00 UTC. It postponed that session three minutes before the spot notice, with a new time to be announced separately.

A Seed Tag is Binance's label for newer, higher-volatility tokens. Users have to pass a risk quiz every 90 days and accept the terms before trading one.

## Why was the PREDICT listing postponed?

Binance did not say. The postponement notice only states that the trading start time "will be postponed until further notice."

The timing is hard to ignore. Binance published its first security incident update at 02:23 UTC, then the PREDICT notice at 02:32 UTC. The exchange's own wording does not link them, so the reason stays unconfirmed.

MEXC followed. It postponed its own PREDICT spot listing and said the new time will be announced once confirmed. MEXC added that deposit and withdrawal timelines for the token remain unchanged.

## What is Predict (PREDICT)?

Predict.fun is a prediction market on BNB Chain. A prediction market is a platform where users buy and sell shares in the outcome of an event, such as a football match or a price move. The share price reflects the crowd's estimate of the probability.

The project is backed by YZi Labs, the fund tied to Binance co-founder Changpeng Zhao. Predict.fun has run sports and crypto markets and launched 15-minute stock up/down markets in September 2026.

PREDICT is the platform's native token. Total supply is fixed at 1 billion. The allocation:

- Ecosystem and community: 51.95%
- Early backers: 26.05%
- Core contributors: 10%
- Treasury: 8%
- Liquidity: 4%

Sixteen percent was set to enter circulation at the token generation event. Contributor and backer tokens carry a 24-month cliff and 36 months of vesting.

A token generation event (TGE) is the moment a project creates and distributes its token. It usually comes before exchange trading starts.

## What happens to the PREDICT airdrop?

It waits. Predict.fun said the TGE and the Season 1 airdrop claim are delayed in line with the Binance Alpha and spot postponements. The project will announce a revised claim date once confirmed.

Season 1 distributes 70 million PREDICT, 7% of supply, to holders of Predict Points. Eligible users could take 35% immediately and forfeit the rest, or take 100% after a 12-month staking vest.

Airdrop claims were set to open at 09:00 UTC on October 30. A Pre-TGE Prime Sale ran on Binance Wallet on October 29 with a 6 BNB cap per user, so some buyers already hold tokens or keys tied to a launch that has not happened.

Binance Wallet's claim form still lists cutoffs at 09:00 UTC on November 5, 12, 19 and 26. Those dates assume a token that is live. Binance has not said whether the delay moves them.

## What should traders watch next?

The new listing date is the first thing. Watch Binance's announcement page and Predict.fun's X account, and treat screenshots and forwarded messages as unverified until one of those two posts it.

The airdrop mechanics come second. Check whether the November claim batches move, and whether the 35% instant option still expires on the original timeline. A delayed TGE can leave a claim window open longer than planned, or shorten it.

Then watch the security incident. Binance has paused withdrawals and is targeting a November 1 restart, with a full proof of reserves due November 2. A listing restart before withdrawals work would be unusual, so the two timetables are worth tracking together.

Finally, watch for fake claim sites. No claim is open right now. Any page asking for a wallet connection or a seed phrase before Predict.fun announces a date is a scam.

## Bottom line

PREDICT was hours from its Binance debut when the exchange pulled the listing. Predict.fun's TGE and airdrop moved with it, and MEXC followed.

There is no new date, no stated reason and no confirmed link to the hack. For anyone holding Prime Sale keys or waiting on the airdrop, the only reliable next step is the official announcement.

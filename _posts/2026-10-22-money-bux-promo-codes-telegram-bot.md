## What Money Bux actually is

Money Bux is a Telegram bot, live since January 2024. You complete small tasks inside it and build a balance in the project's own token, called $BUX and also written $MONEYBUX on some trackers. Inside the app, that balance can be exchanged for TON once you clear the withdrawal threshold.

The earning routes are unglamorous. Watch ads for a set duration and get paid. Join partner Telegram channels. Click through to links. Play mini-games and raffles. Then redeem the daily promo code, which is the cheapest of the bunch.

A $BUX balance isn't a listed token you can trade on an exchange. It exists inside the app, and converting it to TON or USDT goes through the bot's own thresholds.

## What is Binance Word of the Day?

Binance Word of the Day, or WOTD, is a daily word game inside the Binance app under Gifts and Campaigns. It plays like Wordle: guess a hidden crypto-related word, and the app colors the tiles after each try.

Green means the letter is correct and in the right spot. Yellow means the letter is in the word but in the wrong position. Black or grey means the letter is not in the word at all. Read the colors after every guess instead of burning attempts on random words.

## How do I play Binance Word of the Day?

Open the official Binance app, not a third-party site. From the bottom menu tap **More**, then open **Gifts and Campaigns**, then pick **Word of the Day**. You can also go straight to the Word of the Day page on the [official Binance site](https://www.binance.com).

Binance lets you play up to two games a day in this cycle. The second one opens when you tap **Get A New WOTD**, share the link, and get a logged-in user to click it. It is a small referral step, but it doubles your daily attempts.

## How much can you earn from Binance WOTD?

Binance lists 14 BNB for this Start with Binance AI Pro cycle.

The first pool is 4 BNB and goes to users who submit at least five correct answers. Your share depends on your number of right answers against the total from all eligible players.

The second pool is 10 BNB and goes to users who hit five correct answers and also finish a trading task. The task is either $50 in cumulative non-zero-fee trades on Spot, Convert, TradFi, bStocks, or Futures, or $30 in NEWT through Spot or Futures. That pool caps each user at 0.01 BNB.

Rewards are scheduled for the Rewards Hub by November 15, 2026, at 23:59 UTC. Binance says the activity is not available to users in the EEA.

You will still see posts claiming a 500,000 BNB pool. That number is stale copy from an old campaign and does not match the current activity page.

Binance can change the pool, the eligibility rules, or the vouchers at any time without notice. Crypto rewards are taxable in many places, including India, where gains face a 30% flat tax and transfers carry a 1% TDS. Check the campaign page in the app for today's real numbers.

## Why do some users see different Binance WOTD answers?

Binance gives different word lengths, and sometimes different words, to different accounts under the same daily theme. That is deliberate. One shared answer would break the game on day one.

Count your tiles, pick the matching group, and ignore anyone who claims a single universal answer. Themes change weekly and words rotate, so yesterday's list will not carry you through tomorrow.

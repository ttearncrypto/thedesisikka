Upbit Indonesia opened Bubblemaps (BMT) and Nillion (NIL) trading against BTC and USDT at 12:00 WIB on October 29, 2026. Deposits went live 30 minutes after the notice. Neither token got a rupiah pair.

The listing adds four order books: BMT/BTC, BMT/USDT, NIL/BTC, and NIL/USDT. For a local buyer holding rupiah, that means buying bitcoin or tether first, then buying the altcoin.

Upbit Indonesia is licensed and supervised by OJK, the country's financial services authority.

## What is Bubblemaps (BMT)?

Bubblemaps is an on-chain analytics platform that turns token distribution into a visual map. Major holders appear as bubbles, and transfers between wallets appear as connecting lines. Traditional block explorers show one address and its transaction history at a time. Bubblemaps is built to show how wallets cluster.

The platform's Intel Desk lets users submit cases, stake BMT to vote on which investigations get priority, and earn rewards for useful research. That turns on-chain sleuthing into a crowdsourced job rather than a solo hunt.

BMT is the token that runs that loop. It pays for Intel Desk participation, opens advanced tools such as AI cluster analysis and historical token tracking, and carries governance rights. The max supply is 1 billion tokens, split across Solana and BNB Chain. About 757 million BMT were circulating on October 30, according to CoinGecko.

## What is Nillion (NIL)?

Nillion is privacy-preserving compute infrastructure. It lets apps store data, run computations, and handle AI workloads while keeping the underlying data confidential.

The stack runs through a product called the Blind Computer. It has separate modules for storage (nilDB), confidential computing (nilCC), and private AI inference (nilAI). Blacklight is the verification layer. Independent nodes check that workloads running inside trusted execution environments behave as claimed.

NIL pays for Nillion services and rewards Blacklight node operators. The Blacklight L1 mainnet went live on October 5, 2026. Nillion's dashboard listed 24.0 million NIL staked across 42 active nodes on October 30, with an APR of 20.88%. Running a node requires a minimum stake of 70,000 NIL.

NIL is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum. About 513 million NIL were circulating on October 30, against a total supply of 1 billion, per The Block.

## Why are BMT and NIL in the news?

Both tokens got Korean won markets on Upbit in the past week, and both moved hard on the news.

Upbit listed BMT/KRW on October 24. BMT was trading at $0.0461, up 62.93% over 24 hours, when the market opened, according to CoinMarketCap data cited by Koin Bülteni. The token cooled after the first orders cleared and traded near $0.027 on October 30, per CoinGecko.

Upbit listed NIL/KRW on October 27. NIL climbed to $0.13968 in 15 minutes, a gain of more than 26%, then settled near $0.12, Odaily reported. By October 30 it traded near $0.093, according to The Block.

That pattern is familiar for new Upbit fiat pairs. A won market gives Korean retail a direct route into a token, the first prints are thin, and price often fades once the queue clears.

The Indonesian listing is a smaller access change. Both assets already traded against BTC and USDT on Upbit's Korean venue since August 21, 2026. Indonesia adds a new set of users and new order books, but the same two quote assets.

## What should traders watch after the listing?

Liquidity comes first. New pairs can run wide spreads and thin depth for days. Compare the four books before sizing an order, and remember that the BTC pairs add a bitcoin conversion on top.

Watch whether Upbit Indonesia adds BMT/IDR or NIL/IDR. That would matter more for local users because it removes the BTC or USDT leg.

Then watch supply. BMT has a token release scheduled for November 18 for 9.7 million tokens, about 0.97% of total supply, according to CoinGecko. NIL has a release around November 24 of roughly 24.5 million tokens, close to 5% of circulating supply, per CoinMarketCap data. Both can add sell pressure if demand stays flat.

On the Nillion side, track staking and node counts on the project dashboard. More staked NIL and more active nodes point to real use of the network. A flat node count after the L1 launch would be the weaker signal.

## Bottom line

BMT and NIL now trade against BTC and USDT on Upbit Indonesia, with no rupiah pair. Both got a Korean won listing in the past week, both popped on the news, and both cooled before the Indonesian market opened.

The listing is live. The next tests are liquidity in the four new books, the November token releases, and whether Nillion's staking metrics keep growing after its L1 launch.

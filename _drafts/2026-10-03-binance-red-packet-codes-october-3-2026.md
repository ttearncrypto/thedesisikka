## How to redeem a Binance Red Packet code

Open the official Binance app. Tap **More** at the bottom, find **Red Packet** (usually under Rewards Hub or Gifts and Campaigns), paste a code, and hit claim. The reward, usually a small slice of BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, XRP or USDC, lands in your Spot wallet.

## What if a code does not work

Most failures are one of three things. The code expired minutes ago, the daily claim cap on that packet was hit, or you already claimed that batch. Move to the next code on the list and try again. There is no penalty for wrong attempts.

## Where the codes come from

These are distributed through Binance's social channels, livestreams, and community posts, then aggregated by tracker sites like QuikNotes. Community lists are unofficial, so treat each code as "try it" rather than "guaranteed." If a code does not redeem, it is not an official Binance rejection of your account.

## Bottom line

Try the six codes above in order, claim whatever lands, and check back tomorrow for the new batch. Packets usually pay small amounts, but the claims take under a minute each. Codes and availability can change without notice, so verify inside the Binance app.

    <h1 class="page-title">Disclosures &amp; conflicts of interest</h1>
    <p class="page-sub">Crypto media is full of paid shilling. Here is exactly where we stand, in plain terms.</p>
  </header>

  <div class="prose" style="max-width:46rem;">
    <h2>No paid coverage</h2>
    <p>We do not accept payment, tokens, or "sponsorship" in exchange for covering a project, exchange or coin. Press releases and announcements appear only in the clearly labeled Press Releases section, never mixed into reporting. If we ever run sponsored content, it will carry a visible "Sponsored" label and live outside news sections.</p>

    <h2>Holdings</h2>
    <p>Staff and contributors may hold small amounts of Bitcoin and other crypto as part of personal portfolios. We do not publish about a specific token, exchange or fund while holding a position large enough to be influenced by the story, and we never trade against stories we publish. A reminder this can't be independently verified: treat any coverage accordingly.</p>

    <h2>Opinion vs. news</h2>
    <p>Predictions, price targets and scenario analysis are published as <strong>opinion</strong> and labeled as such, with sources listed. Price moves are reported as fact of what happened; forecasts are opinions about what might.</p>

    <h2>Affiliate links</h2>
    <p>Some exchange and how-to guides may contain affiliate links in the future. If they do, we will say so next to the link and disclose it in that article. An affiliate link never changes our ranking, verdict or coverage.</p>

    <h2>Corrections</h2>
    <p>When we get something wrong, we fix it, mark the article as updated, and log it in the public <a href="{{ '/corrections/' | relative_url }}">corrections register</a>.</p>

    <h2>Report a conflict</h2>
    <p>If you believe a story has an undisclosed conflict, tell us at <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a> — we investigate and publish any finding.</p>

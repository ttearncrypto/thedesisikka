    <span class="mono-label">Editorial Policy</span>
    <h1 class="page-title">Our reporting rules, on the record.</h1>
    <p class="page-sub">Trust in crypto media is broken for good reasons. Here's exactly how we operate, so you can hold us to it.</p>
  </header>

  <div class="prose" style="max-width:46rem;">
    <h2>Sourcing</h2>
    <p>Every price move gets a source: an exchange feed, official filing, on-chain data or named statement. If we can't verify it, we don't publish it. Rumors get labeled as rumors, clearly and up front.</p>

    <h2>Independence</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>We don't accept payment for coverage. No paid articles dressed as news, ever.</li>
      <li>If a story involves a sponsor, we disclose it in the story itself, not buried in a footer.</li>
      <li>Affiliate links (if added later) will always be marked.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>No price predictions sold as fact</h2>
    <p>We explain why markets moved. We don't promise where they'll go. Anyone claiming certainty about prices is guessing, and we say so even about our own analysis.</p>

    <h2>Corrections</h2>
    <p>Mistakes get fixed visibly. Factual corrections carry an editor's note at the top or bottom of the story stating what changed. Email <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a> with the link and issue; verified fixes land within days, not weeks.</p>

    <h2>AI use</h2>
    <p>Drafts may be assisted by tools, but every published story is researched, checked and edited by a human who takes responsibility for what's in it.</p>

    <h2>Your rights as a reader</h2>
    <p>You can challenge any story publicly. We answer criticism in public channels rather than deleting comments or blocking accounts for disagreeing with us.</p>
  </div>

  <header class="page-intro">
    <span class="mono-label">Reference</span>
    <h1 class="page-title">The crypto glossary.</h1>
    <p class="page-sub">If a story uses a word we don't define, it should be here. Bookmark this page and come back when the jargon hits.</p>
  </header>

  <div class="prose" style="max-width:46rem;">
    <table>
      <thead><tr><th>Term</th><th>Plain English</th></tr></thead>
      <tbody>
        <tr><td><strong>Altcoin</strong></td><td>Any cryptocurrency that isn't Bitcoin.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>ATH / ATL</strong></td><td>All-time high / all-time low — the highest or lowest price ever recorded.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Blockchain</strong></td><td>A shared digital ledger where transactions are recorded in batches ("blocks") that can't be silently changed.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>DEX</strong></td><td>Decentralized exchange — a marketplace running on code, where you trade directly from your own wallet without a company in the middle.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>DeFi</strong></td><td>Decentralized finance — lending, borrowing, trading and yield built on blockchains instead of banks.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>DIN</strong></td><td>A reference number Indian platforms generate so bank transfers (IMPS/NEFT) match to your account.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Ethereum gas</strong></td><td>The fee you pay to make a transaction on Ethereum; it rises when the network is busy.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Fiat</strong></td><td>Government currency — rupees, dollars, euros — as opposed to crypto.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>FUD</strong></td><td>"Fear, uncertainty, doubt" — scary news, real or planted, that pushes prices down.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>FOMO</strong></td><td>"Fear of missing out" — buying because something is pumping and you don't want to be left behind. Usually a bad reason.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>HODL</strong></td><td>A typo-turned-term for holding crypto long-term instead of panic-selling.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>KYC</strong></td><td>"Know Your Customer" — the identity verification (PAN, Aadhaar, selfie) exchanges legally run.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Liquidation</strong></td><td>When a leveraged position is force-closed because price moved against it and the margin ran out.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>P2P</strong></td><td>Peer-to-peer — trading directly with another person on a matching platform rather than through an order book.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Seed phrase</strong></td><td>The 12–24 recovery words for a self-custody wallet. Whoever has it owns the coins. Never share it.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Stablecoin</strong></td><td>A crypto token designed to hold a fixed value (usually $1), used to move money without volatility.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>TDS</strong></td><td>Tax Deducted at Source — India's 1% deduction on crypto transfers under Section 194S.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>VDA</strong></td><td>"Virtual Digital Asset" — the Indian tax-law name for crypto, taxed at 30% under Section 115BBH.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Wallet</strong></td><td>Software or hardware that holds your private keys — the thing that actually controls your coins.</td></tr>
        <tr><td><strong>Yield farming</strong></td><td>Depositing crypto into a protocol to earn rewards; the yield is compensation for real risk, not free money.</td></tr>
      </tbody>
    </table>
  </div>
